The Saturn Return Is Just a Performance Review

Hot take · evergreen · 4 min read






  
☊︎Sky Link

  

    
Where is this topic in the sky right now? (Written once. Always current.)

    
Calculating…

  






  
☿saturn.htm

  

    


Saturn goes around the Sun in about 29.5 years, so somewhere between 27 and 30 it comes back to the place it stood when you were born. The internet describes this as a crisis. It is a review. The agenda was always going to be: what did you build, what did you only talk about, and what are you still carrying that nobody asked you to.


What actually gets reviewed


    

  • The job you took because it was available.
    • 

  • The relationship you kept because leaving was loud.
    • 

  • The promise to yourself you have rescheduled for years.
    • 



Why the drama is optional


The return is not one day. Saturn approaches, passes, turns retrograde, passes again and settles, which usually takes two to three years. Treating it as a single dramatic night is how people stage a crisis nobody ordered.


Saturn is not punishing you. It is reading the minutes of the meetings you skipped.


The take


Plan for it the way you plan for tax season: boring, early and slightly irritated. The people who sail through are not luckier. They did the paperwork in their late twenties.
