What Mercury rules

      
Mercury is the planet of messages: speech, writing, contracts, short trips, devices and the habit of thinking things through. It is small, close to the Sun and fast, so it changes sign every few weeks. That speed is why it shows up in everyday conversation more than any other planet.

      
Direct, shadow, retrograde

      
Three times a year or so, Earth overtakes Mercury and it appears to run backwards for about three weeks. The weeks on either side, when it covers the same ground twice, are called the shadow.

      

        
PhaseRoughlyGood for

        
          
Directmost of the yearStarting, sending, signing

          
Pre-shadow2 weeksFinishing, backing up

          
Retrograde3 weeksRevising, reconnecting

          
Post-shadow2 weeksRelaunching what you revised

        
      

      
A retrograde is a scheduled review, not a curse. It tells you when to re-read, not what will go wrong.
