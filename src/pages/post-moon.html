The Moon does not know you made a vision board. It will go from new to full to new again in about 29.5 days whether or not the candle was lit, and that is exactly why it is useful.

It is a rhythm, not a wish line

The Moon changes sign every two and a half days or so, which makes it the fastest clock in the sky. It is the planet of mood and routine because it moves fast enough to match both.

  • New Moon: start the small thing, quietly.
  • First quarter: hit the first wall.
  • Full Moon: see what you built.
  • Last quarter: let go of the part that did not work.

The take

The rituals are optional. The tracking is the point. Write down how you feel for three months next to the phase and you will have a better personal forecast than any app, and you will not need a single crystal.

The Moon is not listening. It is keeping time.
