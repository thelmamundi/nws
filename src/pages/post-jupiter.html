Jupiter is called the Great Benefic, and people assume that means gifts. It means growth. Growth is neutral about what it grows.

One sign a year

Jupiter takes about 12 years to circle the Sun and sits in one sign for roughly a year. That makes it a yearly theme: this is the area where opportunity gets louder. It returns to its birth position every twelve years, at 12, 24, 36, 48 and so on.

AgeJupiter return
12First
24Second
36Third
48Fourth

The take

A good Jupiter year is a multiplier. Multiply a habit of showing up and you get a lucky year. Multiply a habit of avoiding things and you get an expensive one. The luck was never in the planet. It was in what you handed it to multiply.

Jupiter does not bring you something new. It brings more of what you already are.
