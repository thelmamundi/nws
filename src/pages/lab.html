▦ASCII Astro Calendar

What is the sky doing this month, and what is it touching in my chart? Printed as one block of text.

Times are in your time zone. Exact moments come from the same sky engine as the rest of the site (good to a few arc-minutes). The n in Ma sqr nSu means your natal Sun. Fonts: the full FIGlet collection that ships with figlet.js (MIT); each font file keeps its author's notes.
