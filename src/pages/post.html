Placeholder post. Mercury looks like it runs backwards three or four times a year. It does not. Earth passes it on the inside track, and for a few weeks it appears to slide the wrong way against the stars.

What actually happens

Every planet does this. Mercury does it fastest because it laps us the most. The table below is the shape of a typical cycle.

StageDaysLooks like
Pre-shadowabout 14Slowing down
Station retrograde1Stands still
Retrogradeabout 21Moves backwards
Station direct1Stands still
Post-shadowabout 14Speeding up

What to do with it

  • Back up the thing you keep meaning to back up.
  • Re-read before you send.
  • Finish old projects before starting new ones.
A retrograde is a timing note, not a verdict. It tells you when to revise, not what will go wrong.
