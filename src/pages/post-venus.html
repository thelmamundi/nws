Venus gets sold as the love planet, and that is the brochure version. The working definition is simpler: Venus is what you value. Love is one of the things people value. So is a good chair.

A planet on a short leash

Venus is never more than about 48 degrees from the Sun, which means in any chart it sits in your Sun sign or at most two signs either side. That is why Venus feels like a personal taste and not a worldview: it can never wander far from who you already are.

Read it as a budget

Venus asksIt sounds like
What is worth it?"I will pay more for that."
Who is welcome?"They can sit with us."
What is too much?"I am fine, thank you."

The take

Every relationship column that opens with Venus in a sign is secretly a spending report. When Venus goes retrograde, about every eighteen months for around forty days, it is not a warning about your ex. It is an audit of your standards.

Love is what Venus does on weekends. The job is knowing what you want.
