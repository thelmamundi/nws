Pluto Was Never Demoted

Hot take · evergreen · 4 min read






  
☊︎Sky Link

  

    
Where is this topic in the sky right now? (Written once. Always current.)

    
Calculating…

  






  
☿pluto.htm

  

    


In 2006 astronomers voted that Pluto is a dwarf planet. The internet mourned. Astrologers, meanwhile, did not so much as blink, because astrology never ran on the astronomers' definition of a planet in the first place. It runs on a body that moves through the sky and does something to your week. Pluto qualifies.


The smallest voice in the room


Pluto takes about 248 years to circle the Sun and spends anywhere from 12 to 31 years in a single sign. That makes it a generational planet: it is less about what happens to you on Tuesday and more about what your whole age group will not shut up about for two decades.

The hot take


Being demoted is the most Pluto thing that could have happened. Power that gets stripped of its title and carries on anyway is the entire myth. The rumour that it matters less now is the same rumour that has always surrounded it.


If you only count what the committee counts, you will miss everything the committee cannot measure.
