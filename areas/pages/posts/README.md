# Posts (index and articles)

The Posts archive and the article pages. Every article has the live Sky Link card and a spinning glyph in the header; the article header title is the one place Super VCR Mono is used.

**Area:** `pages/posts`

## Where it lives
- `src/pages/posts.html`  (the archive)
- `src/pages/post.html`  (Mercury) and `src/pages/post-pluto.html`, `post-saturn.html`, `post-venus.html`, `post-moon.html`, `post-jupiter.html`
- `assets/css/dos-skin.css`  (`.doc-article .doc-title`, the TYPE block)
- `build.js`  (adds `doc-article` to article headers)
- `docs/POSTS.md`  (how to write one)
